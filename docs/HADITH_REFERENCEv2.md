NOTE: Sunan an-Nasa'i 524

The 2:1 shadow ratio for Asr comes from the **Hadith of Jibril**, where the Angel Gabriel led the Prophet ﷺ in prayer over two consecutive days to define the prayer times. On the **second day**, Jibril led the Asr prayer when the shadow of an object was **twice its length**.

### 📜 The Primary Hadith

The most explicit narration is from **Ibn Abbas (may Allah be pleased with him)**:

> The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: "Jibril (peace be upon him) led me in prayer at the House (the Ka'bah) twice. He prayed Zuhr with me when the sun had passed its zenith and (the shadow) was the length of a sandal strap. And he prayed 'Asr with me when the shadow was equivalent in length (to an object). ... **On the next day, he prayed Zuhr with me when the shadow was equivalent in length (to an object). And he prayed 'Asr with me when the shadow (of an object) was equivalent to twice its length.** ... Then he turned to face me, and said: 'O Muhammad! These are the times (of prayers) of the Prophets before you, and the time is between these two times.'"
> — **Sunan Abu Dawud 393** (graded Hasan)

The same hadith is recorded in **Jami' at-Tirmidhi 149**, with nearly identical wording: "Then he prayed Asr when the shadow of everything was about twice as long as it."

### 🧠 How the Hanafi School Reached 2:1

Imam Abu Hanifa (may Allah have mercy on him) derived the Asr start time from this hadith through a specific legal reasoning:

- On the **first day**, Jibril led Zuhr when the shadow was short, and Asr when it was **equal to the object's height (1:1)**.
- On the **second day**, Jibril led Zuhr when the shadow was **already equal to the object's height (1:1)** — the exact moment the other schools consider Asr to begin — and then led Asr when it was **twice the height (2:1)**.

Imam Abu Hanifa concluded that **the 1:1 moment on the second day was still Dhuhr time** (because Jibril led Zuhr then), not Asr. Therefore, Asr cannot begin at 1:1; it must begin at **2:1**.

### 📊 The Two Positions

| Position | Asr Begins When Shadow Equals | Primary Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jumhur (Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali, and the Sahibayn)** | **1× object's height** (mithl) | First day of the Jibril hadith, where Asr was led at 1:1. |
| **Imam Abu Hanifa** | **2× object's height** (mithlayn) | Second day of the Jibril hadith, where Zuhr was led at 1:1 and Asr at 2:1, proving 1:1 is still Dhuhr time. |

The Hanafi position is also supported by a narration from **Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)**, who said: *"When the length of your shadow becomes twice your height, perform the Asr salah…"* (Muatta Malik).

### 🔧 In the Code

In your unified script, `--asr-ratio 2.0` selects the Hanafi convention, while `--asr-ratio 1.0` selects the Jumhur convention. Both are derived from the same authentic hadith; the difference is which moment is treated as the start of Asr.
